Abstract

Reeflect is an interactive game about coral reef conservation and the impact of everyday human actions on marine ecosystems. Through simple gameplay, players experience how different choices can either help protect or harm coral reefs. The game encourages Gen Z to reflect on their daily habits and understand that protecting marine life can begin with small, responsible actions.

SDG 14.5

This game is inspired by the importance of SDG 14.5:

“By 2020, conserve at least 10% of coastal and marine areas, consistent with national and international law and based on the best available scientific information.”

The game highlights the importance of protecting coral reefs and marine habitats, showing how everyday human actions can contribute to healthier ocean ecosystems.

Purpose

The game aims to raise awareness about coral reef conservation and encourage Gen Z to make more responsible everyday choices.
